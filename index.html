About

I am a Research and Evaluation Specialist with the City of Philadelphia's Office of the Chief Administrative Officer. Much of my work focuses on strengthening the “evidence-utilization pipeline” — the processes through which agencies gather resident input, translate it into operationally meaningful recommendations, and use those recommendations to inform service improvements.

In my current role, I support the City of Philadelphia's Better Services PHL initiative, which aims to improve how residents experience City services. My work has focused on developing a Citywide framework for diagnosing common service challenges, understanding behavioral bottlenecks through engagements with residents and frontline staff, and translating those findings into actionable service recommendations.

Before joining the City, I was an Associate Fellow with the Office of Evaluation Sciences at the U.S. General Services Administration, supporting projects in their Labor, Education, and Social Policy portfolio. Before that, I earned a joint doctorate from the Department of Social and Decision Sciences and Psychology at Carnegie Mellon University. My dissertation used surveys and behavioral experiments to understand “gaming” behaviors under non-monetary incentives — specifically, incentives targeting “high virtue” behaviors like academic honesty.

Selected Work

City of Philadelphia · Better Services PHL

Designing service standards to inform Citywide service improvements

I helped develop a set of Citywide service standards — shared benchmarks that define what residents can expect from City services — and designed and validated the service assessment framework used to measure services against them.

William Penn Foundation · Funded research

Evaluating the City's approach to building trust by engaging residents

Supported by a $200,000 grant under the William Penn Foundation's Responsive Local Government objective, this research program examines how residents experience the City's resident engagement initiatives — identifying practices that build confidence in the City's responsiveness and recommending improvements grounded in resident input.
